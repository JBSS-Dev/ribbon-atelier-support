Ribbon Atelier

Ribbon Atelier launches with 300 levels. Unwrap each woven pattern one ribbon at a time. Match three ribbons of the same color to free a spool and uncover the print beneath.

Game help

At the worktable

Which ribbons can I pull?

Follow a ribbon from end to end. If every crossing sits beneath it, tap its raised tab. When all three spools are occupied, choose a color already on the rack.

How do I get out of a tight spot?

Undo your last pull to try a different order. Insight marks a safe move when one is available. You can restart a pattern and retry it as often as you like.

Where are my prints and saved patterns?

Open Cabinet for your prints or Pattern book to revisit a board. The game saves each pull on this device. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it erases the saved game.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every puzzle and its print come with the game. You can finish them all offline.

Will you add more levels?

If enough people download Ribbon Atelier, we’ll add more levels.

How does the age question affect ads?

Players under 18 and players who skip the question get an ad-free game. Adults may see general-audience ads. Everyone plays the same patterns and can earn the same prints.

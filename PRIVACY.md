# Ribbon Atelier privacy policy

Published by ViVa Labs · Updated 24 November 2026

This policy covers the Ribbon Atelier Android game and its support site. You can play the complete puzzle collection offline, without creating an account. For a question about your information or your child’s information, contact [hello@vivalabs.dev](mailto:hello@vivalabs.dev?subject=Ribbon%20Atelier%20privacy).

## Your saved game

Your device stores the ribbons you have pulled, unfinished and completed puzzles, prints, mastery stars, collector upgrades, settings and ad-break counters. These records let you resume your game. ViVa Labs does not receive a copy of your saved game.

To erase these records, open Android **Settings → Apps → Ribbon Atelier → Storage → Clear storage**. The wording varies by device. Uninstalling the game also clears its local records. This resets your collection and unfinished patterns.

## Children and advertising

At initial setup, the game asks about age. You can skip the question. The answer is evaluated on the device. The game keeps only whether this installation is eligible for adult advertising. It does not save or send the age entered.

Players under 18 and players who skip the question play without advertising. The game does not start Google’s advertising or consent services for them. All 300 puzzles and their prints are available. The game has no account registration, chat or public player profiles.

Adult players may see ads between completed patterns or choose to watch an ad for two safe pulls. Every eligible ad request asks Google for child-directed treatment, general-audience content and nonpersonalized advertising. ViVa Labs disables publisher first-party advertising identifiers and does not request access to the Android advertising ID.

## Information used for advertising

For eligible adults, ViVa Labs uses Google AdMob to provide advertising and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage applicable privacy choices. Where a privacy form is required, Google can process your selection. When these services run, Google may receive the following information.

- The device’s IP address, which can indicate an approximate location.
- App and ad interactions, such as opening the game, viewing a video or tapping an ad.
- Diagnostics, such as launch time, app hangs and other performance information.
- Permitted identifiers, such as an app set ID, which Google services may use for analytics or fraud prevention.

Google uses ad-service information to deliver and measure ads, assess performance, and detect invalid traffic or fraud. Ribbon Atelier uses Google’s ad SDK without third-party mediation adapters. It does not request Android location permissions. The Google Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit. Google’s retention periods depend on the data and purpose.

[Google Mobile Ads data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure) [Google partner services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) [Google data retention](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention)

## Support messages and this website

If you email us, ViVa Labs and its email service provider receive your email address and the details you send. We use that information to reply and resolve the issue. Please send only what is needed to investigate your question. A pattern name and Android version are usually enough. Parents and guardians can write on a child’s behalf.

Correspondence remains in the support mailbox until deleted. You can ask us to delete messages you have sent. This support site serves its own images and fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and records visitors’ IP addresses for security. [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Choices and deletion requests

Clearing the game’s storage erases your progress and its saved advertising eligibility setting. Eligible adults can use **Settings → Manage ad privacy** when that option appears to review available choices. Google provides separate privacy controls for information held in its services.

Players, parents and guardians can ask ViVa Labs to review or delete support information or stop further use of a child’s information. Identify the game and the email address used to contact us. We may need to verify your connection to the information before responding.
